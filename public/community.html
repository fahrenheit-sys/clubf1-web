What is Fahrenheit One

Four floors.
One membership.

Fahrenheit One is not a standard gym. It is a complete fitness and wellness ecosystem spread across four purpose-built floors — each with its own focus, working together as one membership. The best-equipped, most purposefully designed fitness destination the Eastern Suburbs has ever seen.

The Location

You're not just joining a gym.

Fahrenheit One sits within the new Hakoah Paddington campus — one of the most significant community developments in the Eastern Suburbs. Pools, tennis courts, pickleball, dining, gardens and family facilities, all connected in one destination.

No other gym in Sydney can offer this. When you join Fahrenheit One, the entire Paddington campus becomes part of your daily routine.
