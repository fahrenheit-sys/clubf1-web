Only at Fahrenheit One

Smart technology. Real coaches. Working together.

Most gyms give you equipment and leave the rest to you. At Fahrenheit One, intelligent training technology and on-floor coaching work as one system — so you're guided from your first visit and supported for every one after it.

Technology · eGym smart training

The world's leading smart training system — trusted by 24,000+ clubs and 7 million active users worldwide — anchors our gym floor.

  • — Smart strength machines that recognise you, then set up, load and adjust automatically
  • — Guided onboarding at the Fitness Hub — goal setting, baseline assessment and body analysis in minutes
  • — BioAge — a single, easy-to-understand score that shows your body's real progress
  • — AI-personalised programs that progress automatically as you do
Included with every membership
Coaching · MyCoach

The human layer to AI-driven training — coaching built into your membership, not sold in blocks of sessions.

  • — A coach who knows you — who recognises you by name, and knows your goals and how you've been training
  • — Someone who asks what the data can't: how are you feeling? what could be working better?
  • — Timely, personal guidance on the floor whenever you train — no bookings required
  • — Working hand-in-hand with your eGym plan — for confidence in your first weeks and consistency for years after
Included for Lifestyle members — yours whenever you wish to use it

Together, MyCoach and eGym combine human coaching with intelligent technology — a structured guidance system no other gym in Sydney can offer.
